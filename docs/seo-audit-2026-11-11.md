# SEO and AI discovery audit — 11 November 2026

## Highest-impact finding

The public canonical site returned `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, follow`, and page metadata also disabled indexing because the production `STORE_LAUNCH_APPROVED` variable was absent. The domain and HTTPS are working. This release sets that production variable to true to permit public search discovery. Preview hosts, private pages, internal search/filter results, untranslated product locales and empty models remain excluded. This is indexing eligibility, not proof of Google indexing.

## Improvements

- Public production indexing enabled; preview checks now shared by page metadata and proxy. Private application routes retain a noindex response header even after launch.
- Vehicle, guide and other content pages get their own Open Graph/Twitter title, description and canonical URL instead of inheriting the home-page preview.
- Published built-in shipping, returns and warranty pages included in sitemaps, with published CMS overrides taking precedence and no duplicate URLs.
- Google may use large image previews and unrestricted text/video snippets. Existing product image URLs and server-rendered Product data remain available.
- Removed duplicate policy-page main headings and automatic “last updated” dates that did not reflect content edits.
- Optional Bing Webmaster verification configuration added alongside Google verification; no fabricated verification tokens.
- Repeatable public-site audit checks all four sitemap partitions, duplicates/origins, representative Arabic/English server-rendered pages without JavaScript, canonical URLs, descriptions, JSON-LD parsing, private noindex and missing-product HTTP 404.

## Existing strengths retained

Stable product URLs, Arabic/English alternates only for actual translations, make/model/year in product copy, manufacturer part numbers, genuine-product and product-specific warranty information, crawlable vehicle links, helpful buying guides, explicit unknown inventory and engine-fitment data. The 10 November read-only catalog audit checked 424 published products / 848 localized metadata sets with no missing registered vehicle names or years; this is historical evidence, not a new full database scan.

## Remaining work requiring business data or external accounts

- Verify ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then submit `https://mopc.store/sitemap.xml`. Supply owned verification tokens through `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` / `BING_SITE_VERIFICATION`, or verify through DNS. No connected account was claimed or submitted by this release.
- Review indexing and actual search impressions after recrawling. AI citations and rankings cannot be guaranteed. Current Google guidance says strong SEO fundamentals and reliable, accessible content also support AI search discovery.
- Earlier catalog audit found missing English names, missing photos, unknown stock/condition and fractional saved prices differing from whole-pound presentation. These are business-data limitations; do not invent translations, stock, images, exact fitment, ratings, or alter commercial prices for schema. Existing merchant eligibility gates remain.
- Review Terms/Privacy and checkout policy readiness in the admin; their public URLs currently return HTTP 200. Search indexing approval does not approve legal terms or bypass checkout/payment gates.
- Connect owned analytics and Merchant Center, verify real shipping/returns and inventory, and inspect Google's rich-result diagnostics. Local JSON validation is not Google rich-result certification.
- Measure field Core Web Vitals through Search Console once traffic is sufficient. This audit does not claim a Lighthouse score or field performance result.

Training-crawler opt-out remains unchanged; ordinary search retrieval remains permitted by the general robots group. No special AI keyword stuffing, mass-generated doorway pages or unsupported schema was added.
