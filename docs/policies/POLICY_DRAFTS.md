# Shipping, returns and warranty policies

Prepared 28 October 2026. Original bilingual wording. The owner approved normal delivery in 2–5 business days, express delivery within 48 hours, product-specific warranties and a callback contact form. Rates are configured independently for each of the 27 governorates and shipping methods. Policies remain CMS drafts until the new frontend is deployed.

### English

Delivery areas and charges
We deliver to the shipping areas enabled in the store. Select your area at checkout to see delivery charges and the EGP order total before confirming. If your area is missing, contact us to check delivery availability before buying.

Shipping methods and timing
Normal shipping is within two to five business days; express shipping is within 48 hours of order confirmation and product availability. Normal business days exclude Fridays and public holidays; express timing is measured in elapsed hours. Choose an enabled method for your governorate at checkout. Any known delay or area restriction will be explained before confirmation.

Recipient details
Provide the recipient name, a reachable phone number and a clear address. Contact us before dispatch where possible if these details or delivery arrangements need changing. Reattempts or cancellation are reviewed according to shipment status and communicated with you.

Checking the shipment
Check packaging condition and visible quantity on arrival. Report visible breakage, leakage or damage to the courier and store, retaining photographs where possible. Do not use a damaged product. Accepting delivery does not waive rights concerning a hidden defect or an incorrect item under the return policy and applicable law.

Contact
For shipment assistance, complete the contact form with your mobile number, order number and issue details; we will contact you. Do not send card details or verification codes.

### English

Return and exchange rights
Subject to statutory exceptions, consumers may request an exchange or return for a refund within 14 days of receipt without giving a reason or incurring expenses. For defective goods, the exchange or return right extends to 30 days from receipt without additional charges. This policy does not reduce other legal rights.

Condition and exceptions
Change-of-mind requests are assessed against item condition, its nature and statutory exceptions. Keep components and packaging where possible. Broken fluid seals, use or fitting may affect change-of-mind eligibility depending on the facts, but do not automatically invalidate a defect or wrong-item complaint. We do not impose a blanket exclusion on all electrical or imported parts.

Submitting a request
Complete the contact form with your mobile number, order number or purchase evidence, part number, reason and receipt date; we will contact you. Photos or a technical report may help where relevant. Retain the part and stop using it if a defect is suspected. Agree return arrangements with us; do not send it to an unconfirmed address.

Wrong or incompatible items
If we supplied a different item from your order or from compatibility we confirmed, provide the vehicle and part details so we can resolve the issue. Where vehicle information supplied was incomplete or incorrect, the request is reviewed against the facts and legal return rights; those rights are not assumed to be automatically lost.

Assessment and refunds
We explain the assessment outcome and exchange or refund arrangements. For defective goods covered by the statutory 30-day right, exchange or repayment is completed within one week of contacting us, without extra charges and using the original payment method. For cash purchases, we agree an appropriate repayment arrangement that preserves your rights. Any active finance transaction requires coordination with its provider; installments are not assumed to cancel automatically.

Disputes
A dispute about defects or return rights may be referred to the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency.

### English

Product warranty
Commercial warranty duration, provider and coverage vary by item and supplier. Available documented terms are stated on the product page or warranty document before purchase. No single duration is promised for every product. The absence of a stated commercial warranty does not remove legal rights concerning defects or nonconformity.

Information for a claim
Complete the contact form with your mobile number, order number or purchase evidence, part number, fault description and when it appeared; we will contact you. Relevant photos, installation details or a proportionate technical report may help identify the cause. Do not publish a VIN in a public comment; share it privately only if a review requires it.

Installation and use
Check compatibility and manufacturer instructions and use a qualified technician. Damage caused by incorrect fitting, misuse, modification or an accident may affect commercial coverage where its connection to the fault is established, without automatically extinguishing legal rights. Ordinary wear alone is not treated as a manufacturing defect; each case is assessed individually.

Assessment and resolution
We explain who will assess the item and the next step, then the appropriate resolution under applicable rights and documented commercial terms. We do not promise replacement, repair or reimbursement of fitting costs before assessing the case, and assessment is not used to reduce an established legal right.

Legal rights take priority
A commercial warranty supplements rather than replaces consumer rights. See the return policy for defective, incorrect or nonconforming goods, including issues reported before a supplier warranty process begins.
