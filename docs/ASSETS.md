## Current screenshot-inspired homepage

The current sunset/SUV hero and four grille-card backgrounds are generated illustrative assets in `public/images/cinematic/`, created using the built-in image generation tool. See [REFERENCE_REDESIGN.md](REFERENCE_REDESIGN.md) for their exact prompts and provenance. They are not actual product photographs or fitment proof. The Unsplash landscape above is the retained legacy hero asset. White manufacturer emblems are sourced from the Simple Icons repository, except the Citroën emblem which comes from the official 2022 logo ([Citroen 2022.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Citroen_2022.svg)); brand marks belong to their respective owners.

## Readable commercial typography

The active storefront fonts are **Tajawal** (Arabic; regular, medium, bold and extra bold) and **Inter** (Latin; variable weights). They are self-hosted as optimized WOFF2 files in `public/fonts/`. Both use the SIL Open Font License; copies are included as `OFL-Tajawal.txt` and `OFL-Inter.txt`. Sources: the Google Fonts repositories at `ofl/tajawal` and `ofl/inter`, with optimized web files obtained through the official Google Fonts CSS service. Cairo is retained as a legacy asset and is no longer selected by the stylesheet.
